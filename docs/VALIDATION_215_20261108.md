## Acceptance

Per vLLM model: four offline groups (contracts, composition, flows, controls),
direct inference, three framework text cases, and three framework Tool/contract
cases: 11 checks. Bedrock adds an IAM identity check: 12 checks. The identity check
does not consume model inference, but may contact STS; its offline suite label
does not mean network-free.

Each Tool case requires one actual add call with operands 19 and 23, value 42,
successful RunResult and valid invariants. Six Tool cases passed in each vLLM
archive. Each vLLM model consumed 10 attempts out of its persistent cap of 20.
The reviewed Bedrock budget recorded 10/20 attempts.

These suites do not establish semantic quality across arbitrary prompts, live
multi-agent graph coverage, long-session reliability, fault-injection coverage,
or compatibility with every provider feature. The known Bedrock/Qwen required
nullable-field omission remains unresolved.
